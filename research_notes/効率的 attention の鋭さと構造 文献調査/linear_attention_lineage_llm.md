# Linear / Kernelized Attention in Language Models (2020–2026): Lineage, Sharpness Loss Mechanisms, and Structure-Free Fixes

Scope note: This file covers linear (kernelized) attention and its gated/delta-rule descendants, the diagnosed mechanisms by which they lose "sharpness" relative to softmax, the structure-free fixes (feature maps, normalization, gating, delta rules, local-softmax windows, periodic softmax layers, distillation), the metrics used, and the (sparse) work on structural priors inside linear attention. SSM theory per se is covered in the sibling note `ssm_mamba_theory_and_models.md`; here Mamba-2 appears only through its linear-attention (SSD) reading. Every entry carries the URL that was actually opened. Items marked **(unverified)** could not be confirmed from the fetched page text and should be checked before citation.

---

## Key Question 1: Verified catalogue of the lineage (per-paper records)

### Takeaway
The lineage runs from kernel feature maps that approximate softmax (Linear Transformer 2020, Performer 2021, cosFormer 2022) → diagnosis papers (Devil/TransNormer 2022, Hedgehog 2024, Zoology/Based 2023–24) → data-dependent gating (RetNet decay → GLA/HGRN2/RWKV-6 gates) → delta-rule state updates (DeltaNet, Gated DeltaNet, RWKV-7, KDA) → distillation from softmax (Hedgehog, SUPRA, Mamba-in-Llama, LoLCATs, Liger, RADLADS). Every production-scale 2024–2026 "linear attention LLM" (MiniMax-01, Qwen3-Next, Kimi Linear, Jet-Nemotron, Samba, Griffin/RecurrentGemma, Nemotron-H-style hybrids) is in fact a hybrid that keeps softmax attention somewhere (periodic full layers and/or sliding windows), and the papers themselves say the reason is retrieval/recall.

### Cited Findings

#### 1.1 Foundational feature-map linear attention (2020–2022)

- **Transformers are RNNs: Fast Autoregressive Transformers with Linear Attention** — Katharopoulos, Vyas, Pappas, Fleuret; ICML 2020. Feature map φ(x)=elu(x)+1 (the cosFormer paper states "The Linear Transformer uses ϕ(·)=elu(·)+1"). Causal form "permits an iterative implementation ... and reveals their relationship to recurrent neural networks"; "up to 4000x faster on autoregressive prediction of very long sequences"; claims "similar performance to vanilla transformers". No failure mode diagnosed in the abstract. — [arXiv 2006.16236](https://arxiv.org/abs/2006.16236); elu+1 attribution from [cosFormer full text](https://ar5iv.labs.arxiv.org/html/2202.08791)
  - Later measured gap of this map (1+elu) vs softmax: WikiText-103 val/test ppl 32.63/34.25 vs vanilla 29.63/31.01 (6-layer, 512-d, Devil paper); GLUE avg 70.00 vs 78.79 — [Devil in Linear Transformer](https://ar5iv.labs.arxiv.org/html/2210.10340); cosFormer's own table: Linear Transformer 28.7/30.2 vs vanilla 24.5/26.2 (16-layer, 1024-d) — [cosFormer](https://ar5iv.labs.arxiv.org/html/2202.08791)
- **Rethinking Attention with Performers** — Choromanski et al. (13 authors); ICLR 2021 (oral). FAVOR+ = "Fast Attention Via positive Orthogonal Random features"; claims "unbiased or nearly-unbiased estimation of the attention matrix, uniform convergence and low estimation variance"; tasks "from pixel-prediction through text models to protein sequence modeling". — [arXiv 2009.14794](https://arxiv.org/abs/2009.14794)
  - Measured LM gap: WikiText-103 75.29/77.65 ppl vs vanilla 29.63/31.01; GLUE 63.41 vs 78.79 — [Devil](https://ar5iv.labs.arxiv.org/html/2210.10340). In Based's controlled ablation (362M, 10B Pile tokens, same window/convs), Performer map: Pile ppl 9.08, AR-slice ppl 8.53, SWDE 8.10, FDA 0.36 vs Taylor map 8.65 / 2.07 / 29.16 / 11.71 — [Based full text](https://arxiv.org/html/2402.18668v2)
- **cosFormer: Rethinking Softmax in Attention** — Qin, Sun, Deng, Li, Wei, Lv, Yan, Kong, Zhong; ICLR 2022. Keeps two softmax properties: "i). non-negativeness of the attention matrix; ii). a non-linear re-weighting scheme that can concentrate the distribution of the attention matrix". Feature map ϕ(x)=ReLU(x) (Eq. 7); re-weighting s(Q'_i,K'_j)=Q'_i K'_j^T cos(π/2·(i−j)/M) (Eq. 10), motivated by locality: "a large portion of contextual dependencies are from neighboring tokens" (citing Clark et al. 2019, Kovaleva et al. 2019). WikiText-103 val/test 23.5/23.1 vs vanilla 24.5/26.2 (16 layers, d=1024); LRA avg 55.23 vs 54.39; text-classification avg 85.25 vs 85.70. — [arXiv abs](https://arxiv.org/abs/2202.08791); [full text](https://ar5iv.labs.arxiv.org/html/2202.08791)
  - Independent re-measurement: LRA 62.11 (Devil paper table) — [Devil](https://ar5iv.labs.arxiv.org/html/2210.10340); Hedgehog LRA table lists cosFormer† 55.23 — [Hedgehog](https://arxiv.org/html/2402.04347); on recall, cosFormer map (feat dim 16) SWDE 19.35 / FDA 7.71 vs Taylor 29.16 / 11.71 in Based's ablation — [Based](https://arxiv.org/html/2402.18668v2)

#### 1.2 Diagnosis papers: dilution, gradients, spikiness, recall (2022–2024)

- **The Devil in Linear Transformer** — Zhen Qin, XiaoDong Han, Weixuan Sun, Dongxu Li, Lingpeng Kong, Nick Barnes, Yiran Zhong; EMNLP 2022. Two failure modes: (1) "unbounded gradients in the attention computation adversely impact the convergence"; (2) "attention dilution which trivially distributes attention scores over long sequences while neglecting neighbouring structures". Formal statement: for softmax |∂p_ij/∂s_ik| ≤ 1/4, whereas for kernel linear attention the bound is 1/(4|s_ik|), and Proposition 3.1 shows for any M>0 there exist q,k pushing the gradient magnitude above M ("the gradient of linear attention has no upper bound"). Dilution metric: "locally accumulated attention" = total attention mass within a neighbourhood of ratio r of the sequence around each token, plotted against neighbourhood size (Fig. 2a), plus attention-map visualizations (Fig. 2b); linear transformers "often trivially distribute attention scores over the entire sequence even in early layers". Fixes: NormAttention O = XNorm(Q(KᵀV)) with RMSNorm, no denominator (gradient bounded by a constant depending on ε, d, and norms); DiagAttention = non-overlapping block-diagonal (local) attention, block size 64, in the first 6 layers → model "TransNormer". Scales: AR LM 6 layers/512-d/8 heads; RoBERTa-base; LRA 2 layers/64-d. Numbers: WT-103 val/test TransNormer T2 29.57/31.01 vs vanilla 29.63/31.01, 1+elu 32.63/34.25, Performer 75.29/77.65; GLUE avg T1 79.38, T2 78.78, vanilla 78.79, 1+elu 70.00, Performer 63.41; LRA avg T1 63.71, T2 64.80, Transformer 57.37, Performer 58.00, cosFormer 62.11. No proposition about dilution and no analysis as a function of sequence length beyond defining neighbourhoods as a fraction of input length. — [arXiv abs](https://arxiv.org/abs/2210.10340); [full text (ar5iv)](https://ar5iv.labs.arxiv.org/html/2210.10340); ACL Anthology entry [2022.emnlp-main.473](https://preview.aclanthology.org/moar-dois/2022.emnlp-main.473)
- **Zoology: Measuring and Improving Recall in Efficient Language Models** — Arora, Eyuboglu, Timalsina, Johnson, Poli, Zou, Rudra, Ré; arXiv Dec 2023 (no venue listed). Gated-convolution models "underperform attention by up to 2.1 perplexity points on the Pile"; "82% of the gap" is attributed to associative recall. Gap attribution: tokens are "AR Hits" if they complete a bigram seen earlier in context but occurring ≤1,250 times in training; AR hits are 6.4% of tokens and account for 82% of the gap; share = Δlog(ppl_AR)·|T_AR| / (Δlog(ppl)·|T|). MQAR definition: sequence of N tokens from vocabulary c; for each query position i check whether some j<i has u_i≡u_j, output u_{j+1}; synthetic experiments use vocab 8,192, lengths 64–512, two-layer models. Theory: Claim 1 "Gated-convolution models with two layers require model dimension to scale at least linearly in sequence length in order to solve associative recall", attention solves it with near-constant dimensionality; Proposition 4.3: attention "even without softmax" solves MQAR with O(c²) parameters, O(Nc²+N²c) time, O(1) layers; Theorem 4.4/4.5 give BaseConv parameter bounds Õ(N log c) and O(t·Nc). Scale: 17 pretrained attention/gated-conv models; a 70M attention model beats a 1.4B gated-conv model on AR; input-dependent sparse-attention hybrids "close 97.4% of the gap to attention". — [arXiv abs](https://arxiv.org/abs/2312.04927); [full text](https://arxiv.org/html/2312.04927)
- **The Hedgehog & the Porcupine: Expressive Linear Attentions with Softmax Mimicry** — Michael Zhang, Kush Bhatia, Hermann Kumbong, Christopher Ré; ICLR 2024. Two properties of softmax that prior feature maps lack: "low-entropy (or 'spiky') weights and dot-product monotonicity" ("when the dot product increases (decreases), the attention weight increases (decreases)"). Both are shown qualitatively: entropy values appear only in figures (Fig. 2, Fig. 4; Fig. 4 caption: associative recall performance "strongly corresponds to lower attention entropy"); monotonicity via plots of attention weight vs trained q·k (Fig. 3). Feature map (Eq. 3): φ_mlp(x)=[exp(w₁ᵀx+b),…,exp(w_dᵀx+b)] (single-layer MLP + element-wise exp); softmax-over-feature-dim normalization variant (Eq. 5, "more stable"); negation mapping (Eq. 6) concatenates exp(Wx+b) with exp(−Wx−b) → ℝ^{2d} (code: `torch.cat([exp(x), exp(-x)])`). Training: "attention weight distillation", cross-entropy between softmax attention weights (soft labels) and normalized linear attention weights (Eq. 4); KL is an appendix alternative. Numbers: WT-103 125M train-from-scratch, Hedgehog "closes the gap between standard Transformers and prior linear attention maps by 68.6%" (Table 7 values 18.6 Transformer … 20.8 Hedgehog; column alignment ambiguous in the fetched text); pretrained GPT-2 conversion (Table 10): GPT-2 28.0, GPT-2 FT 15.8, Hybrid H3 18.5, Hyena 18.5, T2R-GPT-2 19.4, HH-GPT-2 16.7; GLUE finetuned-BERT conversion: Hedgehog recovers 99.3% (avg over 8 tasks) vs T2R 88.9%, T2R-HH 93.5%; LRA avg 59.66 vs Transformer 54.39, Linear Trans. 50.55, Performer 51.41, cosFormer† 55.23; Llama-2 7B SAMSum ROUGE-1/2/L: softmax zero-shot 19.3/6.8/14.9, softmax LoRA 51.1/27.6/43.5, T2R LoRA 2.8/0.0/2.6, Hedgehog LoRA 47.4/23.4/39.1; synthetic AR: softmax, Taylor-exp, Hedgehog 100.0 vs 1+ELU, Performer, cosFormer 17.0 ("prior linear attentions struggle to achieve even 20% accuracy"; "increasing spikiness with t=2 actually solves the task"). KL-to-softmax table (BERT-base, Table 14): Hedgehog (WT-103) 0.35–0.48 vs 1+ELU 1.23–2.49, Performer 1.29–2.59, cosFormer 1.14–2.40; conversion-induced attention KL "remains consistent" from 256 to 4096 tokens (Table 5). — [arXiv abs](https://arxiv.org/abs/2402.04347); [full text](https://arxiv.org/html/2402.04347)
- **Simple linear attention language models balance the recall-throughput tradeoff (Based)** — Arora, Eyuboglu, Zhang, Timalsina, Alberti, Zinsley, Zou, Rudra, Ré; arXiv Feb 2024 (v2 Mar 2025; no venue in metadata; ICML 2024 per common citation **(unverified)**). Feature map: 2nd-order Taylor, φ(q)ᵀφ(k)=1+qᵀk+(qᵀk)²/2 ≈ exp(qᵀk/√d), with q,k projected to d′=16 → D=1+d′+d′²=273. Sliding window: 64 or 128 tokens, "small multiples of 16, up to 64"; a 64–128 window "recovers 90.8% of full softmax attention's recall accuracy at 1e-5× its latency". Theory: Theorem 3.1 — any recurrent model that depends causally on its input requires Ω(N) bits of state to solve MQAR (bounded state entries); Theorems 3.2/3.4 lower-bound BaseConv layers. Pareto plot: recurrent state size (bytes) vs MQAR accuracy; "a fundamental tradeoff between the recurrent state size and recall capacity" (Fig. 8). Scales 360M and 1.3B; 10B/30B/50B Pile tokens. 1.3B/50B (Table 1): Pile ppl All/AR/Other — Transformer++ 6.28/1.65/6.82, Based 6.30/1.71/6.82, Mamba 6.28/1.74/6.78; SWDE/FDA/SQuAD — Transformer++ 76.50/80.47/43.47, Based 64.45/30.40/41.62, Mamba 52.75/18.51/35.92; Based beats Mamba on recall tasks by 10.36 points at 1.3B (abstract: 6.22 avg). Ablation Table 6 (362M, 10B tokens) — see KQ3. — [arXiv abs](https://arxiv.org/abs/2402.18668); [full text](https://arxiv.org/html/2402.18668v2)
- **Linear Transformers with Learnable Kernel Functions are Better In-Context Models (ReBased)** — Aksenov, Balagansky, Lo Cicero Vaina, Shaposhnikov, Gorbatovski, Gavrilov; arXiv Feb 2024 (venue not on page; ACL 2024 **(unverified)**). "a singular, elegant alteration to the Based kernel that amplifies its In-Context Learning abilities", evaluated on MQAR and Pile LM. Specific mechanism (learnable affine/normalization before squaring) not confirmed from the abstract page **(unverified)**. — [arXiv abs](https://arxiv.org/abs/2402.10644)

#### 1.3 Decay/gating lineage (2023–2024)

- **Retentive Network: A Successor to Transformer for Large Language Models (RetNet)** — Sun, Dong, Huang, Ma, Xia, Xue, Wang, Wei; arXiv Jul 2023. Recurrent form s_n = A s_{n−1} + K_nᵀv_n, o_n = Q_n s_n; parallel form o_n = Σ_m γ^{n−m}(Q_n e^{inθ})(K_m e^{imθ})† v_m (xPos); decay matrix D_nm=γ^{n−m}; multi-scale decay γ = 1 − 2^{−5−arange(0,h)}; output MSR(X) = (swish(XW_G) ⊙ GroupNorm_h(heads)) W_O. "RetNet starts to outperform Transformer when the model size is larger than 2B"; 6.7B zero-shot avg 69.51 vs Transformer 66.07, 4-shot 69.76 vs 66.44; "For a 7B model and 8k sequence length, RetNet decodes 8.4× faster and saves 70% of memory". Failure mode (measured by others): data-independent decay cannot forget selectively; recall tasks FDA 2.9 (340M) / 14.3 (1.3B) vs Transformer++ 21.4 / 27.4 (GLA/DeltaNet tables). — [arXiv abs](https://arxiv.org/abs/2307.08621); [full text](https://arxiv.org/html/2307.08621v4); recall numbers from [GLA](https://arxiv.org/html/2312.06635v6)
- **TransNormerLLM: A Faster and Better Large Language Model with Improved TransNormer** — Qin, Li, Sun, Sun, Shen, Han, Wei, Lv, Luo, Qiao, Zhong; arXiv Jul 2023 (rev. Jan 2024). Uses "LRPE together with an exponential decay" explicitly "to avoid attention dilution while allowing the model to retain global interactions"; Lightning Attention (>2× runtime, ~4× memory); gating "for smooth training"; tensor normalization. Scales 385M/1B/7B; claims to "match the performance of state-of-the-art LLMs with Transformer". — [arXiv abs](https://arxiv.org/abs/2307.14995)
- **Lightning Attention-2: A Free Lunch for Handling Unlimited Sequence Lengths in Large Language Models** — Qin, Sun, Li, Shen, Sun, Zhong; arXiv Jan 2024. "we utilize the conventional attention computation mechanism for the intra-blocks and apply linear attention kernel tricks for the inter-blocks"; Triton, IO-aware; "consistent training and inference speed regardless of input sequence length". — [arXiv abs](https://arxiv.org/abs/2401.04658)
- **Gated Linear Attention Transformers with Hardware-Efficient Training (GLA)** — Songlin Yang, Bailin Wang, Yikang Shen, Rameswar Panda, Yoon Kim; arXiv Dec 2023 (v6 Aug 2024; ICML 2024 per common citation **(unverified on page)**). Recurrence S_t = (α_tᵀ1) ⊙ S_{t−1} + k_tᵀv_t = Diag(α_t)S_{t−1} + k_tᵀv_t; gate α_t = σ(x_t W_α1 W_α2 + b_α)^{1/τ}, W_α1 ∈ ℝ^{d×16}, τ=16 ("a temperature term to encourage model to have a slower forgetting rate"). Table 2: 340M/15B tokens Wiki/LMB ppl, zero-shot avg — Transformer++ 28.39/42.69/41.2, RetNet 32.33/49.19/41.0, Mamba 28.39/39.66/41.8, GLA 28.65/43.35/41.5; 1.3B/100B — Transformer++ 16.85/13.44/50.9, RetNet 18.64/17.27/48.9, Mamba 17.06/13.89/50.0, GLA 17.22/14.47/51.0. Recall (FDA/SWDE/SQuAD): 340M — Transformer++ 21.4/42.2/22.1, RetNet 2.9/13.3/27.6, Mamba 2.1/12.4/23.0, GLA 8.1/18.6/27.2; 1.3B — Transformer++ 27.4/66.6/31.5, RetNet 14.3/42.8/34.7, Mamba 6.2/41.4/35.2, GLA 19.9/50.6/42.6. Gate ablation (training ppl): RetNet fixed decay 16.55 → data-dependent scalar gate 15.56 → fine-grained gate 14.77. Length: trained on 2K, GLA and RetNet "can generalize to 18K", Mamba struggles beyond 4K; "the lack of a decay term makes it hard for a model to forget information, which may partly explain the instability of linear attention in long-context tasks". — [arXiv abs](https://arxiv.org/abs/2312.06635); [full text](https://arxiv.org/html/2312.06635v6)
- **RWKV: Reinventing RNNs for the Transformer Era (RWKV-4)** — Bo Peng, Eric Alcaide, Quentin Anthony + 31 others; arXiv May 2023 (venue not on page; EMNLP 2023 Findings **(unverified on page)**). "combines the efficient parallelizable training of transformers with the efficient inference of RNNs", linear attention mechanism, up to 14B ("the largest dense RNN ever trained"). — [arXiv abs](https://arxiv.org/abs/2305.13048)
- **Eagle and Finch: RWKV with Matrix-Valued States and Dynamic Recurrence (RWKV-5/6)** — Bo Peng + 29 others; arXiv Apr 2024. Eagle: "multi-headed matrix-valued states"; Finch: "dynamic recurrence mechanism" (data-dependent); 0.46–7.5B (Eagle), 1.6/3.1B (Finch); 1.12T-token multilingual corpus. No recall/MQAR results on the abstract page. — [arXiv abs](https://arxiv.org/abs/2404.05892)
- **MetaLA: Unified Optimal Linear Approximation to Softmax Attention Map** — Yuhong Chou, Man Yao + 9 co-authors; NeurIPS 2024 (oral). Three conditions for an "optimal" linear approximation: C1 dynamic memory ("store the most important information and forget unimportant ones"), C2 static approximation ("approximate any softmax attention map"), C3 least-parameter approximation. Table 2: linear attention without dynamic decay (RetNet, TransNormer) fails C1; GLA and Mamba meet C1–C2 but fail C3 ("redundant parameters" in K); MetaLA drops K, replaces with 1−α_t, adds "self-augmentation" on the output path to "avoid attention dilution", and an optional short conv. SuperGLUE avg (Table 4): 360M/15B — Pythia 43.21, Mamba 43.96, GLA 42.92, MetaLA 44.05; 1.3B/100B — Mamba 42.83, GLA 43.95, MetaLA 45.81; 1.4B/300B MetaLA 49.22 vs Pythia 44.14. MQAR: MetaLA "outperforms other linear models" (Mamba, GLA, Based, RWKV-4); attention baseline ">99.0". — [arXiv abs](https://www.arxiv.org/abs/2411.10741); [full text](https://arxiv.org/html/2411.10741v1); [NeurIPS page](https://nips.cc/virtual/2024/oral/97971)
- **Gated Slot Attention for Efficient Linear-Time Sequence Modeling (GSA)** — Yu Zhang, Songlin Yang + 10 co-authors; NeurIPS 2024. Extends ABC (bounded-memory slots) with GLA-style gating; "a two-layer GLA linked via softmax"; motivation: linear attention and gated variants "still fall short in recall-intensive tasks", and retaining softmax helps when finetuning pretrained Transformers to RNNs (T2R). No numbers on abstract page. — [arXiv abs](https://arxiv.org/abs/2409.07146)

#### 1.4 Delta-rule lineage (2024–2026)

- **Parallelizing Linear Transformers with the Delta Rule over Sequence Length (DeltaNet)** — Songlin Yang, Bailin Wang, Yu Zhang, Yikang Shen, Yoon Kim; arXiv Jun 2024 (v6 Jan 2025, "Final camera ready"; NeurIPS 2024 **(unverified on page)**). Recurrence S_t = S_{t−1} − β_t(S_{t−1}k_t − v_t)k_tᵀ = S_{t−1}(I − β_t k_t k_tᵀ) + β_t v_t k_tᵀ. Failure mode of plain linear attention: "a purely additive update rule makes it difficult to deallocate past key-value associations", key "collisions" when sequence length L exceeds head dimension d. MAD benchmark (fuzzy recall / in-context recall / noisy recall / selective copy): DeltaNet 35.7/100/100/100 vs GLA 6.9/80.8/81.6/88.6, Mamba 6.7/90.4/90.1/86.3, Transformer 29.8/94.1/86.8/99.6. 340M/15B (Wiki/LMB ppl, avg, SWDE/SQuAD/FDA): Transformer++ 28.39/42.69/41.2/42.2/22.1/21.4; DeltaNet (w. conv) 28.24/37.37/42.1/26.4/28.9/12.8; +Sliding Attn (every other layer) 27.06/38.17/42.1/39.3/32.5/18.8; +Global Attn (2 layers) 27.51/35.04/42.1/42.9/32.1/23.1. 1.3B/100B: Transformer++ 16.85/13.44/50.9/66.6/31.5/27.4; DeltaNet 16.87/12.21/51.6/49.5/37.4/17.2; +Sliding 16.56/11.74/52.1/53.3/43.3/22.3; +2 Global 16.55/12.40/51.8/71.0/43.0/29.8. "both the sliding window and global attention hybrids work well, outperforming the strong Transformer++ baselines"; at 1.3B pure DeltaNet underperforms GLA on recall "because of its smaller state size". — [arXiv abs](https://arxiv.org/abs/2406.06484); [full text](https://arxiv.org/html/2406.06484v6)
- **Gated Delta Networks: Improving Mamba2 with Delta Rule (Gated DeltaNet)** — Songlin Yang, Jan Kautz, Ali Hatamizadeh; ICLR 2025. Recurrence (Eq. 10) S_t = S_{t−1}(α_t(I − β_t k_t k_tᵀ)) + β_t v_t k_tᵀ, α_t∈(0,1) data-dependent gate, β_t writing strength. Diagnosis: Mamba2's gate "uniformly decays all key-value associations at each time step"; DeltaNet "only modifies a single key-value pair at a time", limiting erasure. Hybrids: H1 = GDN + SWA; H2 = Mamba2 + GDN + SWA; SWA window 2K, training length 4K. 1.3B/100B FineWeb-Edu (Wiki ppl / LMB ppl / zero-shot avg): Transformer++ 18.53/18.32/52.25; Mamba 17.92/15.06/53.12; Mamba2 16.56/12.56/54.89; DeltaNet 17.71/16.88/52.14; Gated DeltaNet 16.42/12.17/55.32; H1 16.07/12.12/56.40; H2 15.91/12.55/56.18. S-NIAH-2 (1K/2K/4K/8K): DeltaNet 98.4/45.6/18.6/14.4; Mamba2 99.4/98.8/56.2/17.0; GDN 100.0/99.8/92.2/29.6; S-NIAH-3 (1K/2K/4K): DeltaNet 85.2/47.0/22.4; Mamba2 64.4/47.6/4.6; GDN 86.6/84.2/27.6. LongBench avg: Transformer++ 11.0, Mamba 14.6, DeltaNet 13.6, Mamba2 13.5, Samba 15.9, GDN 16.6, H1 17.8, H2 18.4. — [arXiv abs](https://arxiv.org/abs/2412.06464); [full text](https://arxiv.org/html/2412.06464v3)
- **RWKV-7 "Goose" with Expressive Dynamic State Evolution** — Bo Peng, Ruichong Zhang, Daniel Goldstein + 15 others; arXiv Mar 2025. Eq. 17: wkv_t = wkv_{t−1}(diag(w_t) − κ̂_tᵀ(a_t ⊙ κ̂_t)) + v_tᵀk̃_t, with vector-valued decay w_t, in-context learning rate a_t∈(0,1), normalized removal key κ̂_t, replacement key k̃_t ("generalized delta rule", diagonal-plus-rank-one). Expressivity: "can perform state tracking and recognize all regular languages" (S5 with one layer; conditional on TC⁰≠NC¹). Models 0.1–2.9B, 1.6–5.6T tokens; 2.9B English avg 71.5 vs Qwen2.5-3B 71.4 (but MMLU 55.0 vs 65.7). MQAR: "recall 72.93% at the setting of 256 Key-value pairs" (len 2048, WKV size 8192); passkey perfect to 19,600 (1.5B) / 35,000 tokens (2.9B); PG19 "when processing contexts longer than 10k, the loss began to show an increasing trend"; WKV7 kernel "sensitive to the numerical precision". — [arXiv abs](https://arxiv.org/abs/2503.14456); [full text](https://arxiv.org/html/2503.14456v2)
- **Kimi Linear: An Expressive, Efficient Attention Architecture (KDA)** — Kimi Team, Moonshot AI; arXiv 30 Oct 2025. KDA: S_t = (I − β_t k_t k_tᵀ) Diag(α_t) S_{t−1} + β_t k_t v_tᵀ, o_t = S_tᵀq_t, channel-wise α_t ("each feature dimension maintains an independent forgetting rate") vs GDN's per-head scalar. Hybrid: 3 KDA : 1 MLA (full attention), NoPE in all MLA layers. Ratio ablation (16-layer, validation ppl): 3:1 5.65, 1:1 5.66, 7:1 5.70, 0:1 (pure full attention) 5.77, 15:1 5.82; higher ratios "produced a comparable training loss but led to significantly worse validation performance". 48B total / 3B active; fair comparison at 1.4T tokens: MMLU 73.8 vs MLA 71.6 / GDN-H 72.2; RULER-128k 84.3 vs 81.3 / 80.5; up to 75% KV-cache reduction, 6.3× TPOT at 1M. Stated reason for keeping full attention: "Long-context retrieval remains the primary bottleneck for pure linear attention". — [arXiv abs](https://arxiv.org/abs/2510.26692); [full text](https://arxiv.org/html/2510.26692)
- **Gated DeltaNet-2: Decoupling Erase and Write in Linear Attention** — Ali Hatamizadeh, Yejin Choi, Jan Kautz; arXiv May 2026. ### Inferences
- The field's own vocabulary for the failure has shifted: 2022 "attention dilution / unbounded gradients" (Qin), 2024 "low entropy & monotonicity" (Hedgehog) and "recall–state-size tradeoff" (Zoology/Based), 2024–25 "cannot erase / key collisions" (DeltaNet, GDN), 2025 "smoother G&A heads" (Bick et al.), 2026 "global context collapse: rank ≤ d + entropy→uniform" (MHLA). These are complementary descriptions of one phenomenon: a fixed d×d (or d_k×d_v) state cannot produce an argmax-like read-out over N≫d keys.
- Every verified 2025–2026 production linear-attention LLM keeps softmax layers (Qwen3-Next 3:1, Kimi Linear 3:1, MiniMax-01 7:1, Jet-Nemotron 2 full + 2 SWA / 28, Samba/Griffin via SWA), and each paper's stated reason is retrieval; MiniMax's M2 reversal shows the hybrid itself may still be insufficient for multi-hop reasoning at frontier scale.

## Key Question 2: Stated mechanisms of sharpness loss, formal statements, and metrics

### Takeaway
There are five distinct, partly formal mechanisms in the literature: (i) unbounded gradients from the kernel denominator (Devil, Prop. 3.1); (ii) attention dilution / high entropy because positive feature maps cannot produce spiky, monotone weights (Devil empirical; Hedgehog; PolaFormer Theorem 1; MHLA "Conclusion 2"); (iii) rank of the linear attention map ≤ feature dim d (FLatten; MHLA); (iv) information-theoretic recall limits of a fixed-size state (Zoology Claim 1/Prop 4.3; Based Theorem 3.1 Ω(N) bits; Repeat-After-Me Cor. 2.8; DeltaNet key collisions when L>d); (v) additive updates cannot erase (DeltaNet/GDN). Metrics used: locally-accumulated attention mass, row entropy / PSE, KL or output-MSE to softmax, attention-matrix rank, synthetic recall accuracy (AR, MQAR, MAD, S-NIAH/RULER, passkey, phonebook), AR-slice perplexity, and G&A-head ablation.

### Cited Findings

**Mechanism A — unbounded gradients (kernel denominator).**
- Softmax: |∂p_ij/∂s_ik| ≤ 1/4; kernel linear attention: bound 1/(4|s_ik|), and Proposition 3.1: for any M>0 there exist q,k with gradient magnitude > M; NormAttention removes the denominator and bounds the gradient by a constant depending on RMSNorm ε, d, and the norms of output gradients and values. — [Devil in Linear Transformer](https://ar5iv.labs.arxiv.org/html/2210.10340)
- The 2026 "Exact Linear Attention" preprint restates the pair as "gradient explosion and token attention dilution". — [arXiv 2605.18848](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.18848)

**Mechanism B — attention dilution / lack of spikiness (entropy).**
- Linear transformers "often trivially distribute attention scores over the entire sequence even in early layers"; measured as locally accumulated attention (mass within a neighbourhood of ratio r of the sequence) vs neighbourhood size, area under the vanilla curve ≫ linear (Fig. 2a). No formal proposition. — [Devil](https://ar5iv.labs.arxiv.org/html/2210.10340)
- Softmax uses "low-entropy or 'spiky' attention-weight distributions", prior linear maps "much higher entropy or more uniform distributions"; attention maps are "monotonic over query-key dot products"; AR performance "strongly corresponds to lower attention entropy"; 1+ELU/Performer/cosFormer reach 17.0% on AR vs 100% for softmax, Taylor-exp, Hedgehog; "increasing spikiness with t=2 actually solves the task". — [Hedgehog](https://arxiv.org/html/2402.04347)
- Attention-transfer MSE (LoLCATs) ties to downstream quality, and "Larger MSEs coincide with lower softmax attention entropy, which ... linear attentions struggle to approximate"; later layers up to 200× the MSE of earlier ones. — [LoLCATs](https://arxiv.org/html/2410.10254v3)
- PolaFormer formalizes spikiness: PSE(x) = −Σ(x_i/s)log(x_i/s); Theorem 1: any differentiable element-wise g with g′>0 and g″>0 applied to non-negative features reduces PSE; ReLU and ELU+1 fail the conditions. Linear maps "retain only positive-positive interactions" → "higher entropy". — [PolaFormer](https://arxiv.org/html/2501.15061v2)
- MHLA "Conclusion 2": "As the sequence length N increases, the contribution of each token becomes negligible", weights approach uniformity; stated informally, supported by average row entropy over 500 samples (DeiT-T). — [MHLA](https://arxiv.org/html/2601.07832v2)
- Gather-and-Aggregate: in SSMs the retrieval heads show "smoother attention patterns instead of the sharp token transitions effective G&A requires"; Mamba aggregate heads "attend unnecessarily to neighboring tokens", gather heads "leak attention outside segment boundaries", whereas Transformer aggregate patterns "resembl[e] an argmax-like operation". Ablating one G&A head drops Llama-3.1-8B MMLU 66%→25%; Zamba2-7B: zeroing 9 attention heads 64.3%→34.9%; critical heads are "<0.1% of the total head count". — [Bick, Xing, Gu 2025](https://arxiv.org/html/2504.18574v2)
- cosFormer's reading of softmax's role: non-linear re-weighting "can concentrate the distribution of the attention weights" — their cos re-weighting is a locality surrogate for that concentration. — [cosFormer](https://ar5iv.labs.arxiv.org/html/2202.08791)
- Entropy ceiling reference: for a uniform row the entropy reaches its maximum log N (softmax-scaling paper, EMNLP 2025). — [Information Entropy Invariance](https://arxiv.org/abs/2501.08570v3)

**Mechanism C — low rank of the attention map.**
- rank(φ(Q)φ(K)ᵀ) ≤ min{N,d}; DeiT-Tiny softmax 196/196 vs linear 54/196; depthwise conv restores full rank. — [FLatten](https://arxiv.org/html/2308.00442v2)
- rank(A_lin) ≤ min{rank(Q̃),rank(K̃)} ≤ d, "Regardless of N"; attention rank in linear models "consistently capped by the head dimension (typically d_h≤72)"; multi-head token partition raises the bound to min(n, Σ_b min(n_b,d)). — [MHLA](https://arxiv.org/html/2601.07832v2)

**Mechanism D — recall capacity bounded by state size (formal).**
- Claim 1: two-layer gated convolutions "require model dimension to scale at least linearly in sequence length" to solve AR; Proposition 4.3: attention "even without softmax" solves MQAR with O(c²) parameters independent of N. — [Zoology](https://arxiv.org/html/2312.04927)
- Theorem 3.1: any causal recurrent model needs Ω(N) bits of state to solve MQAR (bounded entries); Pareto plot of state bytes vs MQAR accuracy: "a fundamental tradeoff between the recurrent state size and recall capacity". — [Based](https://arxiv.org/html/2402.18668v2)
- Theorem 2.3: a depth-2 transformer of dimension O(n log D) copies with error < L²D^{−n}; Theorem 2.7: GSSM error > 1 − |S|/D^L; Corollary 2.8: GSSM with mem(S) < L log D − 1 has error > 1/2 on length-L uniform strings. Empirically, Pythia-410M beats 2.8B GSSMs on phonebook lookup once L ≥ 70; largest GSSM gets zero on shuffled strings of length 300. — [Repeat After Me](https://arxiv.org/html/2402.01032v2)
- "A key bottleneck lies in the inability of RNNs to perfectly retrieve information from the context, even with CoT"; adding RAG or a single Transformer layer closes the gap. — [RNNs are not Transformers (Yet)](https://arxiv.org/abs/2402.18510)
- Purely additive S_t = S_{t−1} + v_t k_tᵀ "makes it difficult to deallocate past key-value associations", with key "collisions" when L > d. — [DeltaNet](https://arxiv.org/html/2406.06484v6)
- Scaling-law study: TNL "begins to show results in NIAH only after reaching 1B parameters, achieving a maximum success rate of about 10%" (16K easy mode) vs LLaMA ≈50%. — [Scaling Laws for Linear Complexity LMs](https://arxiv.org/html/2406.16690v1)

**Mechanism E — gating vs delta rule (what each fails at).**
- Mamba2's gate "uniformly decays all key-value associations at each time step"; DeltaNet "only modifies a single key-value pair at a time", limiting how quickly outdated information is cleared. — [Gated DeltaNet](https://arxiv.org/html/2412.06464v3)
- MetaLA: models without dynamic decay (RetNet, TransNormer) "cannot memorize dynamically" (fail C1); GLA/Mamba meet C1–C2 but keep "redundant parameters" (fail C3). — [MetaLA](https://arxiv.org/html/2411.10741v1)
- GDN-2: "the scalar tie between erasing and writing" is the remaining limitation of KDA/GDN. — [GDN-2](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.22791)

**Metrics catalogue (how computed).**
- Locally accumulated attention vs neighbourhood ratio r; attention-map visualization. — [Devil](https://ar5iv.labs.arxiv.org/html/2210.10340)
- Attention entropy (figures only), monotonicity plots (weight vs q·k), synthetic AR accuracy, KL(softmax ‖ linear) on BERT-base per GLUE task (Table 14: Hedgehog 0.17–0.93, 1+ELU 1.23–2.49, Performer 1.29–2.59, cosFormer 1.14–2.40), KL by sequence length 256–4096 (Table 5, "remains consistent"). — [Hedgehog](https://arxiv.org/html/2402.04347)
- Per-head, per-layer attention-output MSE between softmax and linear attention. — [LoLCATs](https://arxiv.org/html/2410.10254v3)
- Positive Sequence Entropy with a provable entropy-reduction condition. — [PolaFormer](https://arxiv.org/html/2501.15061v2)
- Rank of the N×N attention matrix (per layer, DeiT-T) and average row entropy over 500 samples. — [FLatten](https://arxiv.org/html/2308.00442v2); [MHLA](https://arxiv.org/html/2601.07832v2)
- AR-slice perplexity: tokens completing an in-context bigram seen ≤1,250 times in training (6.4% of tokens, 82% of gap); MQAR (vocab 8,192, lengths 64–512). — [Zoology](https://arxiv.org/html/2312.04927); Based reports "AR" vs "Other" Pile ppl columns — [Based](https://arxiv.org/html/2402.18668v2)
- Harder MQAR variant: T∈{256,512,1024}, T/4 KV pairs, random filler tokens, vocab 8192, curriculum. — [Mamba-2](https://arxiv.org/html/2405.21060v1)
- MAD suite (compress / fuzzy recall / in-context recall / memorize / noisy recall / selective copy). — [DeltaNet](https://arxiv.org/html/2406.06484v6)
- S-NIAH 1/2/3 at 1K–8K, LongBench, RULER-128k. — [Gated DeltaNet](https://arxiv.org/html/2412.06464v3); [Kimi Linear](https://arxiv.org/html/2510.26692)
- Phonebook lookup vs phonebook length; passkey retrieval vs position/length; NIAH weighted average at 16K. — [Griffin](https://arxiv.org/html/2402.19427v1); [Samba](https://arxiv.org/html/2406.07522v2); [Scaling Laws for Linear LMs](https://arxiv.org/html/2406.16690v1)
- Real-data recall tasks SWDE, FDA, SQuAD (also NQ/TriviaQA/DROP in some papers). — [Based](https://arxiv.org/html/2402.18668v2); [GLA](https://arxiv.org/html/2312.06635v6)
- G&A head identification by layer pruning + per-head ablation on MMLU and a synthetic KV-retrieval task. — [Bick et al.](https://arxiv.org/html/2504.18574v2)

### Inferences
- For a thesis, the cleanest formal anchors are: Devil Prop. 3.1 (gradient), PolaFormer Theorem 1 (entropy can only be reduced by convex-increasing maps, so ReLU/ELU+1 cannot sharpen), FLatten/MHLA rank ≤ d, Based Theorem 3.1 / Repeat-After-Me Cor. 2.8 (Ω(N) bits for exact recall). The dilution-vs-length claim exists only informally (MHLA Conclusion 2; Devil's neighbourhood-as-fraction definition).
- Metric choice should pair a distributional metric computed against softmax on identical inputs (row entropy or PSE; KL / output MSE as in Hedgehog & LoLCATs) with a behavioural one (MQAR or S-NIAH accuracy vs length), since every paper that measured both found them to co-vary (Hedgehog Fig. 4; LoLCATs MSE–entropy; Bick et al. smooth heads ↔ MMLU collapse).

### Gaps
- No paper gives a closed-form theorem of the form "row entropy of linear attention grows as f(N)"; MHLA argues it informally, and the entropy-vs-N sweep was in an appendix (F.4) not visible in the fetched text.
- Deng–Song–Yuan–Zhou's formal results were not retrievable from the abstract page.
- Hedgehog's numeric entropy values and the exact definition (per-row average?) were not in the extracted text.

## Key Question 3: Which structure-free fixes recover most of the softmax gap at matched compute (papers' own ablations)

### Takeaway
In every controlled ablation found, adding a small local softmax window (64–2K tokens) yields the single largest recall recovery, followed by data-dependent gating + delta rule; feature-map redesign (Taylor, Hedgehog, polynomial) recovers most of the *perplexity* gap but only part of the *recall* gap; distillation from softmax recovers ~78–93% of benchmark quality for 7B–405B models only when it also keeps a sliding window and/or a fraction of softmax layers; a few periodic full-softmax layers (6–25%) recover the rest and are what production systems use.

### Cited Findings

**Feature-map redesign (perplexity ≈ recovered; recall partially).**
- Hedgehog closes 68.6% of the WT-103 gap between softmax and prior linear maps at 125M; GLUE conversion 99.3% recovery vs T2R 88.9%; pretrained GPT-2 conversion 16.7 vs T2R 19.4 vs softmax-FT 15.8; synthetic AR 100% vs 17%. — [Hedgehog](https://arxiv.org/html/2402.04347)
- Based Table 6 (362M, 10B tokens, identical window-64 + convs): Taylor d′=16 Pile ppl 8.65 / AR 2.07 / SWDE 29.16 / FDA 11.71; Performer 9.08 / 8.53 / 8.10 / 0.36; cosFormer d′=16 9.03 / 2.42 / 19.35 / 7.71; cosFormer d′=64 8.82 / 2.18 / 25.47 / 9.07; Taylor d′=32 8.56 / 2.00 / 37.62 / 12.89; "diminishing returns from 24 to 32". — [Based](https://arxiv.org/html/2402.18668v2)
- High-degree polynomial attention replaces softmax "without sacrificing model quality" (PolySketchFormer). — [arXiv 2310.01655](https://arxiv.org/abs/2310.01655)
- Vision analogues: focused function +1.3 top-1 and DWC +2.3 over vanilla linear 70.5 (FLatten); PolaFormer DeiT-T 74.6 vs softmax 72.2. — [FLatten](https://arxiv.org/html/2308.00442v2); [PolaFormer](https://arxiv.org/html/2501.15061v2)

**Normalization (NormAttention) — plus early-layer local blocks.**
- TransNormer (NormAttention + DiagAttention in first 6 layers, block 64): WT-103 29.57/31.01 vs vanilla 29.63/31.01 vs 1+elu 32.63/34.25; GLUE 79.38 vs 78.79; LRA 64.80 vs 57.37. Note the fix is not purely structure-free: half the layers are local block attention. — [Devil](https://ar5iv.labs.arxiv.org/html/2210.10340)

**Gating (data-dependent decay).**
- GLA ablation (training ppl): RetNet fixed decay 16.55 → scalar data-dependent gate 15.56 → fine-grained gate 14.77; recall 1.3B FDA 19.9 (GLA) vs 14.3 (RetNet) vs 27.4 (Transformer++). — [GLA](https://arxiv.org/html/2312.06635v6)
- Scaling-law study: no-decay cosFormer2 worse than TNL on CSR/ppl but better on NIAH at 3B (25.8 vs 11.1); HGRN2 (data-dependent) leads at 7B (30.8). — [Scaling Laws](https://arxiv.org/html/2406.16690v1)

**Delta rule and gated delta rule.**
- 340M: DeltaNet SWDE/FDA 26.4/12.8 vs GLA (w. conv) 24.0/7.3 vs Mamba 12.4/2.1; MAD fuzzy recall 35.7 vs 6.9 (GLA). — [DeltaNet](https://arxiv.org/html/2406.06484v6)
- 1.3B: Gated DeltaNet Wiki ppl 16.42 / avg 55.32 vs Mamba2 16.56 / 54.89 vs DeltaNet 17.71 / 52.14 vs Transformer++ 18.53 / 52.25; S-NIAH-2 at 4K: 92.2 vs 56.2 (Mamba2) vs 18.6 (DeltaNet). — [Gated DeltaNet](https://arxiv.org/html/2412.06464v3)
- Infini-attention Linear+Delta vs Linear memory: ppl 9.67 vs 9.65 (PG19), 1M passkey 100/100/100 vs 96/94/100. — [Infini-attention](https://arxiv.org/html/2404.07143v2)
- Jet-Nemotron block sweep under one frozen backbone: Gated DeltaNet retrieval 69.3 vs GLA 66.6, DeltaNet 67.9, Mamba2 68.9, RetNet 63.7. — [Jet-Nemotron](https://arxiv.org/html/2508.15884v3)
- Kimi ratio ablation shows channel-wise gated delta (KDA) hybrids beating pure full attention on validation ppl (5.65 vs 5.77). — [Kimi Linear](https://arxiv.org/html/2510.26692)

**Local softmax window + linear global (largest single recall gain).**
- Based: window 0→64: Pile ppl 8.91→8.65, FDA 10.16→11.71; 64→128: SWDE 29.16→32.13, SQuAD 25.07→31.84; neither window nor convs: AR ppl 2.29 vs 2.09–2.11 with either; window of 64–128 "recovers 90.8% of full softmax attention's recall accuracy at 1e-5× its latency". — [Based](https://arxiv.org/html/2402.18668v2)
- DeltaNet + SWA every other layer: 340M SWDE 26.4→39.3, FDA 12.8→18.8; 1.3B SWDE 49.5→53.3, SQuAD 37.4→43.3; + only 2 global attention layers: 1.3B SWDE 71.0 (> Transformer++ 66.6), FDA 29.8 (> 27.4). — [DeltaNet](https://arxiv.org/html/2406.06484v6)
- GDN-H1 (GDN + SWA 2K): avg 56.40 vs 55.32 pure GDN; LongBench 17.8 vs 16.6. — [Gated DeltaNet](https://arxiv.org/html/2412.06464v3)
- LoLCATs (Llama 3 8B): Hedgehog + attention transfer, no window: MMLU 23.80; + sliding window 64: 52.77; window without attention transfer: 45.80 — the window is the dominant term. — [LoLCATs](https://arxiv.org/html/2410.10254v3)
- Samba (Mamba + SWA 2048) vs Mamba: 1.7B avg 54.33 vs 51.38; Griffin matches Transformer learning speed on selective copying "with a single local attention layer" and solves phonebook perfectly up to the 1024 window. — [Samba](https://arxiv.org/html/2406.07522v2); [Griffin](https://arxiv.org/html/2402.19427v1)
- Zoology: input-dependent sparse-attention hybrids "close 97.4% of the gap to attention". — [Zoology](https://arxiv.org/abs/2312.04927)

**Distillation from softmax.**
- LoLCATs 5-shot MMLU: 8B 52.8/66.6; 70B 67.70/78.80; 405B 72.20/82.98 (gap closure 77.8% / 78.1% vs no-attention-transfer baseline); ~40M tokens, LoRA r=8. — [LoLCATs](https://arxiv.org/html/2410.10254v3)
- Mamba-in-the-Llama (20B tokens): MT-Bench 8.00 (teacher) → 7.35 (50% attn) → 6.86 (25%) → 6.46 (12.5%) → 5.64 (0%); ppl 2.02 → 2.09 → 2.20 → 3.36. — [Mamba in the Llama](https://arxiv.org/html/2408.15237v4)
- Hedgehog-Llama-2 7B SAMSum ROUGE-1 47.4 vs softmax-LoRA 51.1 vs T2R 2.8. — [Hedgehog](https://arxiv.org/html/2402.04347)
- Liger: 93% recovery at 0.02% of pre-training tokens (intra-layer hybrid). — [Liger](https://arxiv.org/abs/2503.01496)
- SUPRA: "persistent in-context learning and long-context modeling shortfalls" after uptraining. — [SUPRA](https://arxiv.org/abs/2405.06640)

**Periodic full-softmax layers (hybrid ratios).**
- Kimi: 3:1 best (5.65) vs 1:1 (5.66), 7:1 (5.70), 0:1 (5.77), 15:1 (5.82). — [Kimi Linear](https://arxiv.org/html/2510.26692)
- MiniMax-01: 1 softmax per 7 linear blocks; hybrid "surpasses the retrieval and extrapolation capabilities of softmax attention" at small scale. — [MiniMax-01](https://arxiv.org/html/2501.08313v1)
- NVIDIA 8B: 7% attention layers → +2.65 avg over Transformer. — [Waleffe et al.](https://arxiv.org/abs/2406.07887)
- Jet-Nemotron: 2 full + 2 SWA out of 28 blocks; retrieval 74.2 vs Qwen3-1.7B 76.1 at 47× throughput. — [Jet-Nemotron](https://arxiv.org/html/2508.15884v3)
- Qwen3-Next: 3:1 GDN:gated attention. — [HF card](https://huggingface.co/Qwen/Qwen3-Next-80B-A3B-Instruct)
- Caveat from MiniMax M2: hybrids that matched on leaderboards "had clear deficits in complex, multi-hop reasoning tasks" at scale; M2 returned to full attention. — [MiniMax blog](https://huggingface.co/blog/MiniMax-AI/why-did-m2-end-up-as-a-full-attention-model)

### Inferences
- A thesis control-arm ladder consistent with these ablations: (1) vanilla 1+ELU, (2) spiky feature map (Hedgehog or Taylor, matched feature dim), (3) +data-dependent gate (GLA-style), (4) +delta rule (GDN), (5) +local softmax window 64–128 (Based/LoLCATs style), (6) +k periodic full-softmax layers (k≈N/8–N/4). Steps 5–6 are "structure" only in the weak sense of a locality mask; they are the ones that close the recall gap.
- "Matched compute" is rarely FLOP-matched in these papers: most match parameters and tokens; windows add O(Nw) FLOPs and KV cache, and Jet-Nemotron notes KV-cache size, not parameter count, dominates throughput.

### Gaps
- No paper was found that reports *all* fixes under one FLOP-matched budget on the same recall suite; Based Table 6 (feature map × window × conv) and LoLCATs Table 5 (map × window × transfer) are the closest factorial ablations.
- Based Table 6 rows for PosELU/ReLU/Square/Identity maps and windows 16/256 were not in the fetched portion.

## Key Question 4: Does sharpness loss / dilution worsen with sequence length?

### Takeaway
Yes, by information-theoretic necessity for exact recall (Ω(N) state bits; GSSM error > 1/2 once L log D exceeds memory) and empirically (S-NIAH accuracy of GDN falls 100→29.6 from 1K→8K; Griffin/Hawk phonebook failure beyond window/state; TNL NIAH ≤10% at 16K; MiniMax SWA hybrid "degraded noticeably as context length grew"); but the *per-row entropy* increase with N is only argued informally (MHLA), and Hedgehog found conversion KL to softmax "consistent" from 256 to 4096, so the length effect is best framed as a recall/capacity effect rather than a proven entropy law.

### Cited Findings
- Devil defines dilution neighbourhoods as a fraction r of sequence length and does not analyze dependence on N beyond that; TransNormerLLM later adds exponential decay (LRPE-d) "to avoid attention dilution". — [Devil](https://ar5iv.labs.arxiv.org/html/2210.10340); [TransNormerLLM](https://arxiv.org/abs/2307.14995)
- MHLA Conclusion 2: "As the sequence length N increases, the contribution of each token becomes negligible" → near-uniform weights; informal, no proof. — [MHLA](https://arxiv.org/html/2601.07832v2)
- Based Theorem 3.1: Ω(N) bits of state needed for MQAR; Pareto curve of state bytes vs accuracy. — [Based](https://arxiv.org/html/2402.18668v2)
- Repeat After Me Cor. 2.8: GSSM with mem(S) < L log D − 1 has error > 1/2 on length-L copy; "performance of the GSSMs (LSTM and MAMBA) drops to zero almost immediately when increasing the input length"; Pythia-410M beats 2.8B GSSMs once phonebook L ≥ 70; Mamba SQuAD "degrades more quickly with the paragraph length". — [Repeat After Me](https://arxiv.org/html/2402.01032v2)
- DeltaNet: key collisions arise "when the sequence length L exceeds the head dimension d". — [DeltaNet](https://arxiv.org/html/2406.06484v6)
- Gated DeltaNet S-NIAH-2 (1K/2K/4K/8K): 100.0/99.8/92.2/29.6; DeltaNet 98.4/45.6/18.6/14.4; Mamba2 99.4/98.8/56.2/17.0. — [Gated DeltaNet](https://arxiv.org/html/2412.06464v3)
- Scaling laws: TNL NIAH max ≈10% at 16K even at 1B+; LLaMA ≈50%. — [Scaling Laws](https://arxiv.org/html/2406.16690v1)
- Griffin solves phonebook "up to a context length that matches its local attention window size of 1024" and degrades beyond; Hawk "fails as they grow". — [Griffin](https://arxiv.org/html/2402.19427v1)
- RWKV-7 passkey perfect only to 19.6K (1.5B) / 35K (2.9B); PG19 loss rises beyond 10K. — [RWKV-7](https://arxiv.org/html/2503.14456v2)
- Stuffed Mamba: "the maximum context length for accurate retrieval of a 5-digit passkey scales exponentially with the state size"; minimum training length to learn forgetting "scales linearly with the state size". — [Stuffed Mamba, COLM 2025](https://arxiv.org/abs/2410.07145)
- GLA: lack of decay "makes it hard for a model to forget information, which may partly explain the instability of linear attention in long-context tasks"; GLA/RetNet extrapolate to 18K, Mamba struggles beyond 4K. — [GLA](https://arxiv.org/html/2312.06635v6)
- MiniMax: hybrid-SWA "Performance degraded noticeably as context length grew"; Kimi: "Long-context retrieval remains the primary bottleneck for pure linear attention". — [MiniMax blog](https://huggingface.co/blog/MiniMax-AI/why-did-m2-end-up-as-a-full-attention-model); [Kimi Linear](https://arxiv.org/html/2510.26692)
- Counterpoint: Hedgehog's conversion-induced attention KL "remains consistent" from 256 to 4096 tokens (fidelity to softmax, not downstream quality). — [Hedgehog](https://arxiv.org/html/2402.04347)
- Entropy ceiling grows as log N for any row distribution. — [Information Entropy Invariance](https://arxiv.org/abs/2501.08570v3)

### Inferences
- The evidence supports the hypothesis that structure (any mechanism that restores a sharp, position-selective read-out) should help more at longer inputs, with the mechanism being capacity/recall rather than a proven entropy drift; a thesis should therefore report sharpness metrics *stratified by length* (e.g., 256/1K/4K/8K) and pair them with MQAR/S-NIAH at the same lengths.

### Gaps
- No paper provides a measured curve of linear-attention row entropy (or KL to softmax) as a function of N in language models; the closest are MHLA's vision-model entropies and Hedgehog's constant KL over 256–4096.
- Exact Mamba/Hard-ALiBi failure lengths in Repeat-After-Me are in figures, not text.

## Key Question 5: Structural priors (graphs, trees, syntax, locality masks) inside linear attention in language settings

### Takeaway
No paper was found that injects syntactic (dependency/constituency) structure into *linear* attention for language modeling; what exists is (a) locality priors — cosFormer's cos re-weighting, RetNet/TransNormerLLM exponential decay, Devil's block-diagonal early layers, sliding-window hybrids — (b) hierarchical/multi-scale state structure (Log-Linear Attention), (c) the SSD view of Mamba-2 as linear attention with a structured 1-semiseparable mask, and (d) syntax-masked attention only in quadratic softmax Transformers (SG-Net, Syntax-BERT, Dependency Transformer Grammars, StructFormer).

### Cited Findings
- Locality as a prior inside linear attention: cosFormer re-weights by cos(π/2·(i−j)/M) because "a large portion of contextual dependencies are from neighboring tokens". — [cosFormer](https://ar5iv.labs.arxiv.org/html/2202.08791)
- RetNet's data-independent decay D_nm = γ^{n−m} with multi-scale γ; TransNormerLLM's LRPE + exponential decay "to avoid attention dilution while allowing the model to retain global interactions". — [RetNet](https://arxiv.org/html/2307.08621v4); [TransNormerLLM](https://arxiv.org/abs/2307.14995)
- Block-diagonal (local) attention in early layers (DiagAttention, block 64, first 6 layers). — [Devil](https://ar5iv.labs.arxiv.org/html/2210.10340)
- Mamba-2/SSD: the dual form is linear attention multiplied elementwise by a structured mask L (1-semiseparable, cumulative product of a_i), explicitly framed as "structured masked attention". — [Mamba-2](https://arxiv.org/html/2405.21060v1)
- A vision paper reformulates Mamba2's scan as "Linear Attention element-wise multiplied by the structured mask" that "explicitly encodes the sequence adjacency of tokens" (Polyline Path Masked Attention; images only). — [arXiv 2506.15940](https://arxiv.org/html/2506.15940v2)
- Hierarchical state structure: Log-Linear Attention grows the number of hidden states logarithmically with N (multi-scale buckets) for Mamba-2 and GDN. — [Log-Linear Attention](https://arxiv.org/abs/2506.04761)
- Structured matrices to build distance-dependent bias in *softmax* attention (MLR matrices), reporting better LM scaling than standard and sliding-window attention; states that linear attention's "inductive biases ... can be too limiting for real data". — [arXiv 2509.07963](https://arxiv.org/html/2509.07963v1)
- Syntax-guided attention exists only for quadratic attention: SG-Net restricts attention to dependency ancestors (AAAI 2020) — [arXiv 2012.13915](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2012.13915); Syntax-aware local attention masks by dependency-tree distance — [arXiv 2012.15150](https://ar5iv.labs.arxiv.org/html/2012.15150); Syntax-BERT — [arXiv 2103.04350](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2103.04350); Dependency Transformer Grammars (ACL 2024) — [ACL Anthology](https://aclanthology.org/2024.acl-long.84.pdf); StructFormer replaces softmax by sigmoid for dependency-constrained attention — [arXiv 2403.09714](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2403.09714); a 2026 scaled-up study finds syntactic LMs with standard causal attention match or exceed structural-mask variants — [arXiv 2610.16837](https://arxiv.org/html/2610.16837)
- Parallax's "local linear attention" is a nonparametric-statistics notion (local-linear estimator), not a positional/syntactic mask. — [Parallax](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.29157)
- Searches "syntax-guided linear attention dependency tree language model", "linear attention structured mask tree locality prior language modeling 2025", and "\"linear attention\" dependency parse OR syntactic structure kernel language model mask" returned no linear-attention + syntax paper. — [search 1](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2012.13915); [search 2](https://arxiv.org/html/2509.07963v1); [search 3](https://aclanthology.org/2024.acl-long.84.pdf)

### Inferences
- The only structural priors that have been placed *inside* linear attention in language are 1-D locality/decay priors and multi-scale state hierarchies; graph/tree priors remain an open slot, which supports positioning a syntax- or graph-structured linear attention as novel, with cosFormer/RetNet decay and SWA hybrids as the natural structure-light baselines.
- Because SSD shows that any 1-semiseparable mask is compatible with linear-time computation, a tree/graph prior that can be expressed as (or approximated by) such a structured mask is the most tractable route; arbitrary dependency masks would break the semiseparable structure.

### Gaps
- Possible non-English or workshop papers on "syntax-aware linear/kernel attention" were not found via the three searches above; a Semantic Scholar cited-by sweep of Qin et al. 2022 and Hedgehog restricted to "syntax"/"dependency" was not performed (tool budget) and is recommended.
- Log-Linear Attention's exact bucket construction (Fenwick tree) and numbers were not confirmed from the abstract page.
