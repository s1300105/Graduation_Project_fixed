## KQ1. Core lineage: S4 → H3/Hyena → Mamba → Mamba-2 (SSD) → Griffin → hybrids. What each paper did, at what scale.

### Takeaway
The lineage moves from fixed (LTI) diagonal/structured recurrences computed as long convolutions (S4, H3, Hyena) to input-dependent ("selective") recurrences computed with hardware-aware scans (Mamba), and then to the state-space-duality view in which a selective SSM with scalar decay is a *masked linear attention* whose mask is a 1-semiseparable matrix (Mamba-2/SSD). That duality is what makes Mamba-2 kernels matmul-based and what later lets Chimera/Hydra replace the causal 1-D mask with a graph- or bidirection-derived mask.

### Cited Findings (catalogue; one bullet per paper)
- **Efficiently Modeling Long Sequences with Structured State Spaces (S4)** — Gu, Goel, Ré; ICLR 2022. Mechanism: continuous-time linear SSM x' = Ax + Bu, y = Cx with HiPPO-initialised A, computed as a global convolution (LTI) with a Cauchy/Woodbury trick; first model to solve Path-X on LRA. **[arXiv ID and details from author knowledge; not re-fetched this session]** — [arXiv 2111.00396](https://arxiv.org/abs/2111.00396)
- **Hungry Hungry Hippos: Towards Language Modeling with State Space Models (H3)** — Fu, Dao, Saab, Thomas, Rudra, Ré; ICLR 2023. Mechanism: two stacked SSMs with multiplicative gating (shift-SSM + diagonal SSM) designed to perform associative recall / induction; FlashConv kernel; hybrid "H3 + 2 attention layers" already found necessary to match Transformers on LM. **[details from author knowledge; not re-fetched]** — [arXiv 2212.14052](https://arxiv.org/abs/2212.14052)
- **Hyena Hierarchy: Towards Larger Convolutional Language Models** — Poli, Massaroli, Nguyen, Fu, Dao, Baccus, Ermon, Rudra, Ré; ICML 2023. Mechanism: implicitly parameterised long convolutions interleaved with data-controlled (element-wise) gating; subquadratic attention replacement. **[details from author knowledge; not re-fetched]** — [arXiv 2302.10866](https://arxiv.org/abs/2302.10866)
- **Mamba: Linear-Time Sequence Modeling with Selective State Spaces** — Gu & Dao; arXiv Dec 2023, COLM 2024. Mechanism: selective SSM (S6): B, C, Δ are functions of the input, breaking time-invariance; hardware-aware parallel scan with kernel fusion/recomputation (no convolution mode). Finding: "Mamba achieves 4-5× higher inference throughput than a Transformer of similar size" (no KV cache). Scale: 130M–2.8B on the Pile (300B tokens); checkpoints mamba-130m/370m/790m/1.4b/2.8b and mamba-2.8b-slimpj (600B SlimPajama) — [arXiv 2312.00752](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2312.00752); checkpoint list from [state-spaces/mamba README](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/state-spaces/mamba/main/README.md)
- **Transformers are SSMs: Generalized Models and Efficient Algorithms Through Structured State Space Duality (Mamba-2)** — Dao & Gu; ICML 2024. Mechanism: State Space Duality (SSD): a selective SSM with scalar-times-identity A_t equals a masked (linear) attention Y = (L ⊙ C Bᵀ) V with L a 1-semiseparable (cumulative-decay) mask; SSD algorithm is chunked, matmul-based, "2-8x faster than Mamba", supports tensor parallelism and a grouped-value-attention head structure. Kernel microbenchmark (single A100 80GB PCIe, state size 64): "crossing over at sequence length 2K and 6× faster at sequence length 16K" vs FlashAttention-2. Scale: mamba2-130m/370m/780m/1.3b/2.7b plus baselines transformerpp-2.7b and mamba2attn-2.7b, all Pile 300B — [arXiv 2405.21060](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2405.21060); HF doc summary of SSD/2-8x: [HF Mamba2 docs](https://huggingface.co/docs/transformers/en/model_doc/mamba2); checkpoints: [state-spaces/mamba README](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/state-spaces/mamba/main/README.md). Caveat (secondary review): the 2K/16K figures are a core-layer microbenchmark, not end-to-end; a full Mamba-2 model can still train slower than a parameter-matched Transformer at ~2K — [search summary citing a review](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2503.18970)
- **Griffin: Mixing Gated Linear Recurrences with Local Attention** (De et al., Google DeepMind, 2024) and **RecurrentGemma** (2024). Mechanism: RG-LRU gated linear recurrence; block pattern "two residual blocks with a recurrent block, followed by a residual block that incorporates the local MQA attention block" (2:1 recurrent:local-attention), local attention window 2K. RecurrentGemma sizes ≈2.68B and 8.58B total params, pre-trained on 2T tokens — [Google developers blog](https://developers.googleblog.com/en/gemma-explained-recurrentgemma-architecture/); [RecurrentGemma arXiv 2404.07839](https://arxiv.org/html/2404.07839v2); Griffin paper **[arXiv 2402.19427, not re-fetched]** (https://arxiv.org/abs/2402.19427)
- **Hydra: Bidirectional State Space Models Through Generalized Matrix Mixers** — Hwang, Lahoti, Puduppully, Dao, Gu; NeurIPS 2024. Mechanism: "matrix mixer" view; bidirectional Mamba parameterised as a *quasiseparable* matrix (non-zero upper triangle), argued "strictly more expressive than mixer matrices of addition-based bidirectional SSMs". Results: +0.8 GLUE over BERT, +2% ImageNet top-1 over ViT as a drop-in attention replacement — [arXiv 2407.09941](https://arxiv.org/abs/2407.09941v1)
- **Mamba-3** (2026): listed by the FLA README as arXiv 2603.15569 and the mamba README says "Mamba-3: Requires a source install from the latest tree". **[content not read; unverified]** — [FLA README](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/fla-org/flash-linear-attention/main/README.md); [state-spaces/mamba README](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/state-spaces/mamba/main/README.md)
- **Log-Linear Attention** (2025): a log-linear Mamba-2 variant whose custom kernel "outperforms FlashAttention-2 (forward + backward) at sequence lengths beyond 8K" — [arXiv 2506.04761](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2506.04761)

### Inferences
- The SSD identity (SSM = masked linear attention with a structured mask) is the single most useful fact for a fusion-layer design: whatever structure you want (tree, DAG, graph) can be expressed as a different mask L, and the question becomes whether that L admits a linear-time recurrence (it does for DAGs; see KQ4).
- The hybrid necessity was visible as early as H3 (2 attention layers kept) and is re-confirmed by every later generation; "pure SSM" LLMs are the exception (Falcon-Mamba, Codestral Mamba), not the rule.

- S4/H3/Hyena/Griffin details were not re-fetched in this session (budget); arXiv IDs are from memory and should be spot-checked.
- Mamba-3 (2026) content not reviewed.

## KQ2. Expressivity and failure modes: what exactly can SSMs not do that softmax attention can, and vice versa?

### Takeaway
Three distinct limits are now theorem-backed: (i) **state tracking** — diagonal/selective SSMs (and Transformers) sit in TC⁰ and cannot compose permutations (S₅) or even compute parity when transition eigenvalues are restricted to [0,1]; (ii) **copying / in-context retrieval** — any fixed-state model is bounded by state size, while a 2-layer Transformer copies exponential-length strings; (iii) **length generalisation** — "state collapse" beyond training length. The converse is also formal: SSMs implement exact, length-generalising solutions for *star-free* languages and bounded hierarchy that Transformers only approximate. Selection (input-dependent Δ, B, C) is necessary but not sufficient; what unlocks state tracking is *negative eigenvalues* or *non-diagonal, input-dependent transitions*.

### Cited Findings
- **The Illusion of State in State-Space Models** — Merrill, Petty, Sabharwal; ICML 2024 (arXiv revised Mar 2025). Formal result: S4, Mamba and related SSMs have expressive power "close to transformers", within TC⁰, hence cannot solve permutation composition (S₅ word problem) and, by extension, "track chess moves ... evaluate code, or track entities in long narratives". Empirical: S4 and Mamba fail state tracking; a small change — input-dependent (non-diagonal) transition matrices — lets SSMs learn these tasks — [arXiv 2404.08819](https://arxiv.org/abs/2404.08819v3); [ICML page](https://icml.cc/virtual/2024/poster/34075)
- **The Expressive Capacity of State Space Models: A Formal Language Perspective** — Sarrof, Veitsman, Hahn; NeurIPS 2024. Formal: SSMs express star-free languages, a range of counter languages, and bounded hierarchical structure (bounded Dyck) "with optimal memory even without simulating a stack"; "SSMs implement straightforward and exact solutions to problems that transformers struggle to represent exactly" (star-free state tracking), length-generalising. Limit (Thm 2): a nonnegative gate parameterisation (exp/sigmoid gates) restricts capacity (parity impossible). Verified on Mamba — [arXiv 2405.17394](https://arxiv.org/abs/2405.17394v1); [NeurIPS proceedings](https://proceedings.neurips.cc/paper_files/paper/2024/hash/485e0981e81766248b61fd1ec43c118f-Abstract.html)
- **Unlocking State-Tracking in Linear RNNs Through Negative Eigenvalues** — Grazzi, Siems, Franke, Zela, Hutter, Pontil; ICLR 2025 (oral). Formal: finite-precision LRNNs with all non-negative transition eigenvalues cannot solve parity; LRNNs whose transition matrices are products of (I − vvᵀ)-type matrices with eigenvalues in [−1,1] can learn any regular language. Empirical: extending Mamba's and DeltaNet's eigenvalue range to [−1,1] solves parity and improves state tracking (DeltaNet [−1,1]: 97.1% modular arithmetic vs 31.4% for [0,1]); stable pretraining at 1.3B — [arXiv 2411.12537](https://arxiv.org/abs/2411.12537); [ICLR page](https://iclr.cc/virtual/2025/poster/29442)
- **DeltaProduct** (Siems et al., 2025; arXiv 2502.10297): "multiple steps per token to achieve superior state-tracking" (products of Householder transitions). Listed as a supported kernel in FLA. **[venue unverified]** — [FLA README listing](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/fla-org/flash-linear-attention/main/README.md)
- **Repeat After Me: Transformers are Better than State Space Models at Copying** — Jelassi, Brandfonbrener, Kakade, Malach; ICML 2024 (PMLR 235:21502–21521). Formal: a two-layer transformer copies strings of exponential length; "generalized state space models" are limited by fixed-size latent state. Empirical: transformers train faster and length-generalise better on synthetic copying; pretrained Pythia beats Mamba of similar size on copying/retrieval from context; authors recommend hybrids with an attention-like retrieval mechanism — [arXiv 2402.01032](https://arxiv.org/abs/2402.01032); [PMLR](https://proceedings.mlr.press/v235/jelassi24a.html)
- **RNNs are not Transformers (Yet): The Key Bottleneck on In-context Retrieval** — Wen, Dang, Lyu; ICLR 2025. Formal/empirical: CoT helps RNNs but does not close the gap; the bottleneck is in-context retrieval (associative recall, IsTree on graphs); adding RAG **or a single Transformer layer** lets RNNs+CoT solve all polynomial-time problems — [ICLR proceedings](https://proceedings.iclr.cc/paper_files/paper/2025/hash/79dc391a2c1067e9ac2b764e31a60377-Abstract-Conference.html); [arXiv 2402.18510](https://arxiv.org/abs/2402.18510v1)
- **Simple linear attention language models balance the recall-throughput tradeoff (BASED)** — Arora et al.; ICML 2024. Finding: a fundamental tradeoff between recurrent state size and recall quality; "attention excels at recall but maintains a full KV-cache; recurrent models (e.g., H3, Mamba, RWKV) struggle to perform recall as well". BASED (linear attn + sliding window) dials the state size to trace the Pareto frontier; +10.36 acc points over Mamba on recall-intensive tasks at matched perplexity; 24× FA2 decode throughput for 1.3B. Evaluates on the MQAR synthetic recall task from Zoology (Arora et al. 2023) — [arXiv 2402.18668](https://arxiv.org/abs/2402.18668); Zoology **[arXiv 2312.04927, not re-fetched]** (https://arxiv.org/abs/2312.04927)
- **Stuffed Mamba: State Collapse and State Capacity of RNN-Based Long-Context Modeling** — Chen et al. (THUNLP), 2024. Finding: Mamba/RWKV trained <10K tokens fail beyond training length due to "state collapse" (a few outlier channels explode), attributed to over-parameterised state overfitting to training length; three training-free mitigations allow >1M tokens; passkey-retrieval length is "an exponential function of the state size"; a Mamba-2 370M reaches near-perfect passkey at 256K — [arXiv 2410.07145](https://arxiv.org/abs/2410.07145v1)
- **Jamba (empirical failure mode of pure Mamba at scale)**: pure-Mamba 1.3B "performed much worse on IMDB, QuAC, and NarrativeQA, and often ignored the required answer format (e.g., '3/10' instead of 'Positive')"; hybrid matched attention-only; authors attribute this to attention enabling induction-style copying and found induction-like heads in the attention layers — [Jamba arXiv 2403.19887v2](https://arxiv.org/html/2403.19887v2)
- **Role of selection in Mamba** (what selection buys): the Jamba paper found "Jamba without explicit positional information performed about the same as Jamba with RoPE", i.e. the Mamba layers supply implicit position — [Jamba](https://arxiv.org/html/2403.19887v2); Chimera formalises this: the SSM mask L = (I − A)⁻¹ "encodes the topology, and no position embeddings are needed" — [Chimera arXiv 2510.12111](https://arxiv.org/html/2510.12111)
- **"Transformers are SSMs" duality consequences**: SSD places Mamba-2, RetNet and linear attention in a common "Structured Masked Attention" family Y = (L ⊙ C Bᵀ) V; Chimera restates that for a sequence L is the resolvent of a weighted directed line graph — [Chimera](https://arxiv.org/html/2510.12111); [Mamba-2](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2405.21060)

### Inferences
- **SSM cannot (vs softmax attention can):** (a) exact copying/retrieval of arbitrary earlier tokens beyond what the fixed state holds (Jelassi; Arora; Wen) — this is the dominant practical failure and the reason every hybrid keeps a few attention layers; (b) non-solvable-group state tracking (S₅) in one pass, shared with Transformers (Merrill), and parity when gates are nonnegative (Sarrof, Grazzi); (c) extrapolation beyond training length without mitigation (Stuffed Mamba).
- **SSM can (vs softmax attention struggles):** exact, length-generalising star-free state tracking (e.g., flip-flop-like memory) and bounded Dyck with optimal memory (Sarrof); implicit positional information with no PE (Jamba, Chimera); linear-time / constant-memory decoding.
- For a fusion layer, the actionable design levers are: (1) keep at least one softmax layer (Wen's "single Transformer layer suffices" result), (2) if state tracking matters, allow negative eigenvalues (Grazzi) or non-diagonal input-dependent transitions (Merrill), (3) size the state to the recall you need (Arora).

### Gaps
- Zoology (MQAR) paper itself was not fetched; the MQAR definition is cited only via BASED.
- Exact theorem statements (e.g., Sarrof Thm 2, Merrill Thm numbering) were not extracted from full texts.
- I found no 2026 paper that overturns the TC⁰ result for diagonal SSMs; absence here is not evidence of absence.

## KQ3. Hybrid architectures: attention:SSM ratios, placement, and the ablations behind them

### Takeaway
Across 2024–2026 the empirical sweet spot is a *small* attention fraction: Jamba 1:7 (1:3 was equal at 1.3B), Nemotron-H ≈8% of layers, Zamba one shared block every ~6 Mamba blocks, Griffin 1:2, Falcon-H1 1/8 of channels, Hymba ≈1:2.12 attention:Mamba parameters, Qwen3-Next/Kimi Linear 1:3, MiniMax-01 1:7. Ablations consistently show gains saturate quickly with more attention and that *where* attention is (induction/recall) matters more than how much. This supports using a "sparse structural softmax + SSM" hybrid as a legitimate control: the literature's hybrids are precisely "a few (often local/sliding-window) softmax layers + mostly SSM".

### Cited Findings
- **Jamba: A Hybrid Transformer-Mamba Language Model** — Lieber et al. (AI21), 2024. Architecture: Jamba blocks of l=8 layers, attention:Mamba = 1:7, MoE every e=2 layers, 16 experts top-2; 52B total / 12B active; fits one 80GB GPU; 256K context. Ablation (1.3B, 250B tokens): both hybrids beat pure attention and pure Mamba; "1:3 and 1:7 performed about the same" → 1:7 chosen for efficiency. 7B/50B-token: hybrid (no MoE) > pure attention > pure Mamba; MoE helps further. Stability: at 7B scale loss spikes traced to large Mamba activations, fixed by RMSNorm on internal activations. KV cache at 256K (16-bit): Jamba 4GB vs Mixtral 32GB vs LLaMA-2 128GB. Throughput ≈3× Mixtral (A100, 8K ctx, bs16; and 4×A100 at 128K). Apache 2.0 — [arXiv 2403.19887v2](https://arxiv.org/html/2403.19887v2); LoRA example on model card: [ai21labs/Jamba-v0.1 discussion #49](https://huggingface.co/ai21labs/Jamba-v0.1/discussions/49)
- **Samba: Simple Hybrid State Space Models for Efficient Unlimited Context Language Modeling** — Ren et al. (Microsoft); ICLR 2025. Mechanism: layer-wise interleave of Mamba, Sliding Window Attention and MLP. Scale: 3.8B / 3.2T tokens. Length: trained on 4K, "extrapolates to a 256K context length with perfect memory recall on Passkey", improved perplexity up to 1M zero-shot; 3.73× throughput vs GQA Transformer at 128K prompts — [arXiv 2406.07522](https://huggingface.co/papers/2406.07522); [ICLR 2025 PDF](https://proceedings.iclr.cc/paper_files/paper/2025/file/84a7fc24ed52e8eff514c33e8ac76ea3-Paper-Conference.pdf). The 2048-token window size was **not confirmed** in sources retrieved.
- **Zamba: A Compact 7B SSM Hybrid Model** — Glorioso et al. (Zyphra), 2024. Mechanism: Mamba backbone + one *shared* attention module whose weights are reused across calls (inputs concatenated with early-layer activations) to cut KV-cache; 7B, 1T tokens; a survey places the shared block every six Mamba blocks — [arXiv 2405.16712](https://arxiv.org/abs/2405.16712v1); survey placement (secondary): [arXiv 2503.10665](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2503.10665)
- **Zamba2-7B** (Zyphra, Oct 2024): Mamba2 blocks replace Mamba1; ~7.4B params; 2T tokens + ~100B high-quality anneal; Apache 2.0 (gated download); HF transformers class Zamba2 exists — [HF Zyphra/Zamba2-7B](https://huggingface.co/Zyphra/Zamba2-7B); [HF Mamba2 docs linking Zamba2](https://huggingface.co/docs/transformers/en/model_doc/mamba2)
- **Hymba: A Hybrid-head Architecture for Small Language Models** — Dong et al. (NVIDIA), 2024. Mechanism: attention heads and SSM heads run *in parallel within the same layer* (hybrid-head). Ablation: "performance improves as the ratio of attention parameters increases and gradually saturates when the parameter ratio of attention to Mamba reaches 1:2.12"; they stopped adding attention heads due to memory. Weights: nvidia/Hymba-1.5B-Base and -Instruct, NVIDIA Open Model License, commercial use permitted — [arXiv 2411.13676](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2411.13676); [HF Hymba-1.5B-Base](https://huggingface.co/nvidia/Hymba-1.5B-Base). (Meta-tokens, cross-layer KV sharing, SWA-in-most-layers details **[from author knowledge; not re-verified]**.)
- **Nemotron-H: A Family of Accurate and Efficient Hybrid Mamba-Transformer Models** — NVIDIA, 2025. Mechanism: attention layers ≈8% of total, evenly dispersed; 4 of 52 layers in 8B, 10 of 118 in 56B; 47B is a distilled/compressed 56B — [arXiv 2504.03624](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2504.03624)
- **Falcon-H1: A Family of Hybrid-Head Language Models** — TII, 2025. Mechanism: attention and Mamba-2 heads in parallel within each mixer block, head counts set independently; ablation over fully-parallel / semi-parallel / fully-sequential picked semi-parallel "SA_M" (attention and SSM together, then MLP); channel allocation (α_S, α_A, α_M) = (2/8, 1/8, 5/8) i.e. SSM:attention:MLP = 2:1:5; "increasing attention channels deteriorates performance". Sizes 0.5B, 1.5B, 1.5B-deep, 3B, 7B, 34B; up to 256K context; HF transformers class FalconH1 — [arXiv 2507.22448](https://arxiv.org/html/2507.22448v1); [HF paper page](https://huggingface.co/papers/2507.22448); [GitHub tiiuae/Falcon-H1](https://github.com/tiiuae/Falcon-H1)
- **Griffin / RecurrentGemma**: 2 recurrent blocks : 1 local-MQA block, 2K window — [Google blog](https://developers.googleblog.com/en/gemma-explained-recurrentgemma-architecture/)
- **Qwen3-Next (2025)**: 3 Gated DeltaNet blocks : 1 gated full-attention block; 80B-A3B. **Kimi Linear (Oct 2025)**: Kimi Delta Attention (GDN refinement) with 3:1 linear:MLA layers. **MiniMax-01**: 7:1 lightning-attention:softmax. **Granite 4.0 (IBM, Oct 2025)**: Mamba-2/attention hybrid reported as 9:1 (secondary, conflicting source — verify on IBM card). **Nemotron 3 Nano (Dec 2025)**: Mamba-2 + sparse MoE with self-attention in a small subset of layers — [Raschka hybrid-attention gallery](https://sebastianraschka.com/llm-architecture-gallery/hybrid-attention/); [Raschka DeltaNet chapter](https://sebastianraschka.com/llms-from-scratch/ch04/08_deltanet/); Granite 9:1: [Trilogy AI brief (secondary)](https://trilogyai.substack.com/p/news-brief-the-resurgence-of-us-open); KDA kernel arXiv 2510.26692 and Gated DeltaNet arXiv 2412.06464 per [FLA README](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/fla-org/flash-linear-attention/main/README.md)
- **Distillation-made hybrids**: MOHAWK (Bick, Li, Xing, Kolter, Gu 2024) distils Phi-1.5 into Mamba-2 (Phi-Mamba, 3B tokens) and a hybrid keeping 4 attention layers (5B tokens) — [arXiv 2408.10189](https://arxiv.org/abs/2408.10189v2). "The Mamba in the Llama" (Wang et al. 2024) reuses attention projection weights to distil Zephyr-7B / Llama-3 8B into hybrid Mamba/Mamba2 with 20B tokens — [arXiv 2408.15237](https://arxiv.org/html/2408.15237v4). Jet-Nemotron (NVIDIA, 2025): PostNAS freezes MLPs of a pretrained Transformer and *searches which full-attention layers to keep*, then designs JetBlock (dynamic conv linear attention); 2B/4B built from Qwen2.5-1.5B/3B; 47× generation throughput vs Qwen3-1.7B at 64K on H100 — [arXiv 2508.15884](https://arxiv.org/html/2508.15884v3); [project page](https://hanlab.mit.edu/projects/jet-nemotron). "Priming: Hybrid State Space Models From Pre-trained Transformers" (2026) **[not read; unverified]** — [HF paper 2605.08301](https://huggingface.co/papers/2605.08301.md)
- **Theory for why few attention layers suffice**: one Transformer layer (or RAG) added to an RNN removes the in-context-retrieval bottleneck for all P problems with CoT — [Wen et al.](https://proceedings.iclr.cc/paper_files/paper/2025/hash/79dc391a2c1067e9ac2b764e31a60377-Abstract-Conference.html); attention's job in hybrids is induction/copying — [Jamba](https://arxiv.org/html/2403.19887v2)

### Inferences
- Ratios are not fundamental constants: Jamba's 1:3≈1:7, Hymba's saturation, and Falcon-H1's "more attention hurts" all say the curve flattens early, so the control hybrid in a text+graph experiment can use a *small* number of softmax layers (e.g., 1 of 8) and still be representative of the literature.
- Two placement families exist: *sequential interleave* (Jamba, Samba, Nemotron-H, Qwen3-Next) and *parallel heads in one layer* (Hymba, Falcon-H1). Falcon-H1's ablation preferred semi-parallel; this is directly relevant if the fusion layer runs "structural softmax" and SSM side by side rather than stacked.
- The "sparse structural softmax" idea (softmax restricted to graph edges) resembles the *sliding-window* attention used in Samba/Griffin/BASED in that both bound the KV state; the literature treats such local softmax as the natural partner of a global SSM state.

- Granite 4.0 ratio rests on a secondary brief; Nemotron 3 Nano details only from a blog gallery.

## KQ4. Available pretrained Mamba-family LLMs and fine-tuning tooling (HF / PEFT / LoRA)

### Takeaway
Open Mamba-family text models span pure-SSM (Falcon-Mamba-7B, Codestral Mamba 7B, state-spaces Mamba/Mamba-2 ≤2.8B) and hybrids (Jamba, Zamba/Zamba2, Hymba-1.5B, Nemotron-H, Falcon-H1 0.5–34B, Bamba-9B). All have HF transformers classes; LoRA works, but the evidence says to apply it to the *linear projections* (in_proj/out_proj/x_proj/embeddings), not to the SSM parameters, and prefix/prompt tuning is ineffective. Fast kernels (mamba-ssm, causal-conv1d) are Linux/CUDA-only; HF ships a pure-PyTorch fallback.

### Cited Findings (model table)
- **state-spaces Mamba / Mamba-2** (130M–2.8B; Pile 300B; base only; no context length listed). ## KQ5. Structure in the recurrence: tree / DAG / graph entering the SSM transition or operator (beyond node-ordering graph-SSMs)

### Takeaway
There are now three *principled* ways to put structure into the SSM operator itself rather than into the scan order: (1) **Chimera** — the SSD mask L becomes the resolvent (I − A)⁻¹ of a selective, input-dependent weighted adjacency matrix; linear-time recurrence over a DAG in topological order, truncated Neumann series for cyclic graphs (quadratic), Mamba-2 recovered on the directed line graph; (2) **tree scans** — GrootVL/MambaTree build an input-dependent spanning tree and propagate state by DP along it in linear time; (3) **pLSTM** — Source/Transition/Mark gates on the line-graph of a DAG with multi-dimensional associative scans. Multi-directional raster scans (VMamba, Vim) are the generic fallback for non-sequential data but decompose into 1-D passes, cost k× per layer, and break patch continuity. I found no paper that runs a tree-structured Mamba over syntax trees/ASTs for code.

### Cited Findings
- **Chimera: State Space Models Beyond Sequences** — Lahoti, Marwah, Puduppully, Gu; TMLR (Oct 2025), arXiv 2510.12111. Mechanism: in SMA form Y = (L ⊙ C Bᵀ) V, for a sequence L = (I − A)⁻¹ = Σ Aⁱ where A is the weighted adjacency of a directed line graph with sub-diagonal entries = decays a_t. Chimera builds A from an arbitrary graph: B, C, V, Δ are input-dependent (linear / local graph conv / Swish) as in Mamba-2; A_ij = exp(−(Δ_i + Δ_j + Δ'_ij)/3) (node selectivity at both ends + optional edge term); row-wise normalisation with learned Ψ and scale γ keeps ‖A‖<1 so the Neumann series converges. **DAG**: adjacency nilpotent ⇒ finite sum ⇒ one pass in topological order, h_i = Σ_{j∈parents(i)} A_ij h_j − B̄_i v_i (normalised by 1/√|p(i)|), cost O(|V|+|E|); squaring trick (I+A)(I+A²)(I+A⁴)… in O(log diameter) matmuls but materialises the quadratic matrix. **Undirected/cyclic**: undirected line graph → two directed line graphs; grid → four directed grid DAGs ("2D-DAG"); general graphs: exact resolvent is cubic, approximation truncates at k = diameter by repeated squaring, worst-case quadratic, preserves reachability (Prop. 4.6). **Mamba-2 is the special case** of a directed line graph. Results: GLUE 83.93 (110M, MLM on C4) vs BERT-Base 83.2; ImageNet-1k Chimera-ViT-B 81.4% (82.1 EMA) vs ViT-B 78.8; 22M ablation: 2D-DAG 77.8 vs Fwd&Rev-1D 76.5 vs Fwd-1D 73.8; LRGB (<500k params): Peptides-func 0.7021 AP, Peptides-struct 0.2433 MAE, PascalVOC-SP 0.4460 F1, COCO-SP 0.3977 F1 (NeuralWalker higher on three, Graph Mamba higher on Peptides-func; approximate variant 0.6979/0.2420/0.4353/0.3833 ≈ GraphGPS+Transformer). Stated limitations: cubic exact / quadratic approx on general graphs; current implementation ≈1.5× slower than Transformer baselines; DAG normalisation relies on i.i.d. Gaussian assumption; graph experiments drop the gated MLP — [arXiv 2510.12111 (HTML)](https://arxiv.org/html/2510.12111); [abs](https://arxiv.org/abs/2510.12111)
- **GrootVL / MambaTree: Tree Topology is All You Need in State Space Model** — Xiao, Song, Huang, Wang, Song, Ge, Li, Shan; arXiv Jun 2024; NeurIPS 2024 poster as "MambaTree". Mechanism: input-dependent tree generated from spatial relationships and input features (a search summary describes a minimum spanning tree over a 4-connected grid); state propagated along the tree with a "linear complexity dynamic programming algorithm"; applied to classification/detection/segmentation and to text via LLM fine-tuning; code github.com/EasonXiao-888/GrootVL — [arXiv 2406.02395](https://arxiv.org/abs/2406.02395); [NeurIPS poster](https://neurips.cc/virtual/2024/poster/94853)
- **pLSTM: parallelizable Linear Source Transition Mark networks** — Pöppel et al. (JKU Linz); NeurIPS 2025, arXiv 2506.11997. Mechanism: extends linear RNNs to general DAGs with Source, Transition and Mark gates acting on the *line graph* of the DAG; parallelisation via associative scans in multiple dimensions; P-mode (directed propagation) vs D-mode (diffusive distribution) to control vanishing/exploding over long DAG distances; code ml-jku/plstm_experiments — [arXiv 2506.11997](https://arxiv.org/html/2506.11997v1); [NeurIPS page](https://neurips.cc/virtual/2025/poster/120092)
- **Hydra** (bidirectional via quasiseparable mask) — see KQ1; relevant as the non-causal 1-D operator a fusion layer would need — [arXiv 2407.09941](https://arxiv.org/abs/2407.09941v1)
- **STree: Speculative Tree Decoding** (2025): accumulates SSM state-transition matrices along a *token tree* for speculative decoding — tree structure in the transition, but for decoding trees, not syntax trees — [arXiv 2505.14969](https://arxiv.org/html/2505.14969v2)
- **TreeGPT** (2025) argues Mamba "centers on sequential processing, lacking the specialized mechanisms necessary for effective tree structure modeling" and builds an attention-free TreeFFN for ASTs instead — [arXiv 2509.05550](https://arxiv.org/html/2509.05550v2)
- **Multi-directional scans — VMamba: Visual State Space Model** (Liu et al. 2024). SS2D "Cross-Scan" = 4 directional 1-D scans; ablation: Cross-Scan > Unidi/Bidi/Cascade; "only Cross-Scan and Cascade-Scan exhibit two-dimensional (2D) priors"; Bidi-Scan has global ERF but no 2D prior; DWConv "plays a critical role in establishing 2D priors"; Cascade-Scan "significantly constrained by its relatively slow computational pace". Critiques: per-layer cost ≈k× the 1-D case (k=4); SS2D "leads to a loss of patch continuity"; both VMamba and 2DMamba "decompose the operation into sequential horizontal and vertical 1D passes" rather than a native 2-D recurrence — [arXiv 2401.10166](https://arxiv.org/html/2401.10166v3); surveys (secondary): [Mamba in Vision survey 2410.03105](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2410.03105), [medical Mamba survey 2410.02362](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2410.02362); native-2D critique: [arXiv 2412.16146](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2412.16146v3). Vision Mamba (Vim) bidirectional scan **[arXiv 2401.09417, not re-fetched]**
- **Graph-SSM lineage by node ordering (already catalogued elsewhere; for cross-reference)**: Graph Mamba (Behrouz & Hashemi 2024) — main design problem is choosing a node/token ordering for the scan — [arXiv 2402.08678](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2402.08678); survey of scan strategies (bidirectional, parallel, etc.) — [Exploring Graph Mamba survey 2412.18322](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2412.18322)

### Inferences
- **Chimera is the direct template for an SSM-based fusion layer over text+graph**: treat text as a directed line graph and the graph as a DAG (or 2-DAG decomposition), build one adjacency A with input-dependent decays, and run a single topological-order recurrence. Because Chimera's DAG operator is *exactly* structured masked attention with mask (I − A)⁻¹, the natural control is masked softmax attention over the same adjacency ("sparse structural softmax"); the comparison then isolates (linear mask + state) vs (softmax + explicit pairwise scores).
- Cycles are the cost cliff: Chimera's own numbers show exact resolvent cubic, approximation quadratic and ~1.5× slower than a Transformer; for undirected graphs expect to either orient edges (DAG-ify, as Chimera does for grids) or accept quadratic cost — which is also what sparse softmax costs on the edge set.
- Tree scans (GrootVL) are cheaper than Chimera on general graphs (linear) but need a spanning tree, discarding non-tree edges; pLSTM's gating on the line graph is the most general DAG formulation but has no LLM-scale checkpoints.
- Multi-directional raster scans should be regarded as a baseline, not a solution: their own authors show gains come partly from DWConv, and critics show they are compositions of 1-D passes.

### Gaps
- No work found applying tree-structured SSM scans to syntax trees / code ASTs ("Mamba on trees for parsing"); TreeGPT explicitly claims the gap. Mark as an open niche.
- GrootVL's precise DP recurrence and its text-task numbers were not extracted (abstract-level only).
- No independent replication of Chimera's LRGB numbers found (TMLR Oct 2025; too recent).
- Chimera code availability / kernel status not checked.

## KQ6. Practical: kernels, throughput/memory vs FlashAttention-2, platform and GPU constraints

### Takeaway
Published head-to-heads are on data-centre GPUs: Mamba-2 SSD ≈ FA2 at 2K and ~6× faster at 16K (A100, core layer only); FLA's chunked kernels beat FlashAttention at B=1/T=8K on GB200 but lose at B=4/T=2K. I found **no published Mamba-vs-FA2 numbers on 24GB consumer GPUs**. mamba-ssm/causal-conv1d are officially Linux + NVIDIA CUDA (ROCm with patches); Windows only via community wheels; FLA is Triton-based (NVIDIA/AMD/Intel), Windows not mentioned.

### Cited Findings
- **mamba-ssm** (state-spaces/mamba README, 2026): requirements Linux, Python 3.10+, PyTorch 1.12+, NVIDIA GPU + CUDA 11.6+ for `selective_scan_cuda`; AMD ROCm supported (ROCm 6.0 needs `rocm_patch/rocm6_0.patch`; not needed ≥6.1); default pip install is pure-Python without `selective_scan_cuda`; `MAMBA_KEEP_CUDA_BUILD=TRUE` installs prebuilt CUDA/HIP wheel or compiles; `--no-build-isolation` required; no compute-capability minimum stated; Windows not mentioned; Mamba-3 requires source install — [README](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/state-spaces/mamba/main/README.md)
- **Windows**: an open Windows support request remains in the issue tracker; community wheels exist (e.g., Torch 2.7.1/CUDA 12.8/Py3.10; another set for torch 2.4/CUDA 12.4 targeting compute capability 6.0–9.0); Triton "officially only supports Linux", community Windows Triton builds tied to specific CUDA versions — [issue tracker mirror](https://issues.ecosyste.ms/hosts/GitHub/repositories/state-spaces%2Fmamba/issues); [community Windows wheels](https://github.com/Redtash1/Files/releases/tag/v2.7.1); [CSDN build guide (secondary)](https://blog.csdn.net/yyywxk/article/details/136071016)
- **flash-linear-attention (fla-org)**: "platform-agnostic and verified on NVIDIA, AMD, and Intel"; install extras `[cuda]`, `[rocm]`, `[xpu]`, `[npu]`, `[cpu]`; Triton comes via the PyTorch wheel; supports Mamba2, Samba, Gated DeltaNet, DeltaNet, DeltaProduct, KDA, RWKV6/7, Mamba3 (2026) and ~40 others; HF-compatible with per-layer `attn` hybrid plan; GB200 benchmark (CUDA 12.9, PyTorch 2.9): fwd B1/T8192/H96/D128 chunk_gdn 1.265 ms vs flash_attn 3.753 ms; fwd B4/T2048/H16/D128 chunk_gdn 0.753 vs flash_attn 0.346 ms; fwd+bwd B1/T8192 chunk_gdn 4.738 vs 15.371 ms; fwd+bwd B8/T2048/H32/D256 chunk_gdn 8.644 vs 6.748 ms. Windows not mentioned — [FLA README](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/fla-org/flash-linear-attention/main/README.md). Older listings required PyTorch ≥2.3–2.5, Triton ≥3.0, causal-conv1d ≥1.4 — [rwkv-fla PyPI](https://pypi.org/project/rwkv-fla/0.1.dev20240905)
- **Mamba-2 vs FA2**: crossover at 2K, 6× at 16K (A100 80GB PCIe, state 64, core layer) — [arXiv 2405.21060](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2405.21060); PyTorch blog on fusing Mamba-2 kernels — [pytorch.org](https://pytorch.org/blog/accelerating-mamba2-with-kernel-fusion/)
- **End-to-end hybrid throughput**: Jamba ≈3× Mixtral (A100 80GB, 8K, bs16; 4×A100 at 128K) — [Jamba](https://arxiv.org/html/2403.19887v2); Samba 3.73× GQA Transformer at 128K prompts — [Samba](https://huggingface.co/papers/2406.07522); Mamba 4–5× Transformer inference throughput — [Mamba](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2312.00752); BASED 24× FA2 decode at 1.3B — [BASED](https://arxiv.org/abs/2402.18668)
- **Consumer GPUs**: FlashAttention repo reports 2.5–4.5× speedup over standard attention on RTX 3090 (lower GDDR6X bandwidth) — [flash-attention repo mirror](https://github.com/h2oai/flash-attention); a 2026 scheduler paper ran Mamba-1.4B on 4×RTX 3090 but reports no FA2 comparison — [arXiv 2604.10597](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2604.10597); RTX 4090 ≈1.3–1.8× RTX 3090 FP16 training throughput — [Lambda](https://lambda.ai/blog/nvidia-rtx-4090-vs-rtx-3090-deep-learning-benchmark)
- HF Mamba2 note: the CUDA-kernel path is *slower* for prefill than uncompiled `torch_forward` (3–4×) because of warm-up/CPU overhead — [HF Mamba2 docs](https://huggingface.co/docs/transformers/en/model_doc/mamba2)

### Inferences
- At the sequence lengths typical of a graduation-project fusion layer (≤4K), published kernel data predict rough parity or an FA2 advantage; the SSM win appears only ≥8–16K. Memory (no KV cache / O(1) state) rather than speed is the stronger argument at short length.
- For a 24GB Linux box the dependable stack is PyTorch + Triton + FLA (Mamba2/GDN chunk kernels) with mamba-ssm/causal-conv1d as optional accelerators; on Windows use WSL2.

### Gaps
- No published Mamba-2 vs FlashAttention-2 benchmark on RTX 3090/4090-class GPUs; would need to be measured locally.
- causal-conv1d's minimum compute capability not retrieved from its README (community wheels suggest 6.0–9.0, unverified).
- Mamba-3 kernel requirements not reviewed.
